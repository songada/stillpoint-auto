Performance car rental in the San Francisco Bay Area.

Stillpoint Auto rents two drivers' cars from Fremont: a 2023 BMW M2 with the six-speed manual, for the street or the track, and a track-prepared 2018 Mercedes-AMG GT R for track days.

Delivery is available anywhere in the Bay Area. Track days are arrive and drive at WeatherTech Raceway Laguna Seca, Sonoma Raceway and Thunderhill Raceway Park, with delivery to the track included.
