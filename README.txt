STILLPOINT AUTO — CONCEPT REDESIGN (demo)
================================================

Open index.html in any modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox).
No server, no build step, no internet needed — all photos, videos and
sounds are included in this folder.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- index.html — the whole redesigned site (single page).
- assets/img/ — photography from the owner's SmugMug gallery
  ("101126 AMG M2"), web-optimized.
- assets/video/ — walkaround films of the actual cars (720p):
  m2-walkaround.mp4 (1:13), amg-walkaround.mp4 (1:02).
- assets/audio/ — exhaust recordings used for the interactive cockpit:
  m2-exhaust.mp3, amg-exhaust.mp3 (royalty-free performance-engine
  recordings, Free Sounds Library, CC BY 4.0 — a demo stand-in for
  each car's voice, not a recording of these two exact cars).

TRY THE INTERACTIVE COCKPIT
1. Scroll to "Get in the driver's seat."
2. Pick BMW M2 or Mercedes-AMG GT R.
3. Press START ENGINE (browsers only allow sound after you click).
4. Hold HOLD TO REV — or hold the Space bar — and watch the rev
   needle sweep to the redline. The exhaust sound follows the revs.
5. Switching cars stops the engine; press START again for the other car.
